The Artist

Meet Neena

“
I believe henna is more than an art form—it’s a conversation between the skin and the soul. Every design I create is a celebration of life’s most meaningful moments, honoring each transition with grace, beauty, and intention.

For over three decades, I have been transforming stories into intricate works of art through the timeless tradition of mehndi. What began as a childhood fascination—watching my mother and family members create beautiful henna designs—blossomed into a lifelong passion and devotion. Every piece I create is drawn freehand, making each design completely original and uniquely tailored to the individual.

Every design is a bespoke masterpiece, meticulously crafted to reflect each bride’s unique story, cultural heritage, and personal style. My work is never mere decoration—it is a deeply personal art form that honors tradition while embracing contemporary elegance.

I am deeply committed to preserving the purity of this ancient art. My henna paste is handcrafted using only 100% natural, organic ingredients—pure henna powder, essential oils, fresh lemon juice, and water. Free from chemicals and artificial additives, it offers a safe, authentic, and beautiful experience while producing rich, long-lasting stains.

With more than 185 five-star reviews, my reputation speaks through the hands of countless brides who have trusted me with their most cherished day.
