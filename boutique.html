Beyond the Henna Cone

The Boutique

An exclusive home-based Indian boutique in Cary — designer clothing, jewelry and accessories, chosen piece by piece. Ask me what’s in stock.

“
I believe true elegance lies in the details. A beautifully woven saree, an intricately designed blouse, a statement piece of jewelry, or the perfect accessory can transform not only an outfit, but the way a woman feels when she wears it.

My journey with Indian artistry extends beyond henna. For more than 20 years I have served women through my exclusive home-based boutique, curating designer sarees, lehengas, dresses and blouses alongside high-quality gold-plated jewelry, exquisite oxidized pieces and carefully selected accessories.

As a home-based boutique, I offer a more personal and private shopping experience. I enjoy getting to know my clients, understanding their style and the occasion they are dressing for, and helping them discover pieces that feel distinctive and uniquely their own.

My eye for fashion is deeply connected to my work as a henna artist. Both are rooted in my love for Indian culture, craftsmanship and tradition, while embracing the elegance of contemporary design. Whether I am creating a bridal mehndi design or helping a client find the perfect saree, my goal is the same — to celebrate her individuality and make her feel truly special.

I have been fortunate to be part of countless weddings, celebrations and festivals, and it is deeply meaningful to know that pieces from my boutique have become part of memories families treasure for years.
